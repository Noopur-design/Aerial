How to Care forWool and Cashmere

Natural fibres are remarkably resilient — if you give them a little time. Here is how our studio cares for wool and cashmere.

Wash less

Wool is naturally odour-resistant. Air your knit overnight after wearing and you will rarely need to wash it more than a few times a season.

Always fold

Hanging stretches knitwear out of shape. Fold it loosely and store it with cedar blocks to keep moths away.

Rest your tailoring

Give a wool blazer a day off between wears, on a wide shaped hanger. A gentle steam will release creases and refresh the fibres.
